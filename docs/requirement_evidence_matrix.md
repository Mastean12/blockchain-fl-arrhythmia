## Recent evidence additions (2026-10-25)

The diagnostic work added concrete evidence about the low-utility baseline without changing the frozen historical protocol:

- The centralized CNN under the frozen MIT-BIH split is constrained by severe 15-class imbalance; the rare classes are sparsely supported in validation/test, so macro-F1 remains low even when the model learns the dominant class well. See [`model_diagnostics.md`](model_diagnostics.md).
- The repo now keeps a controlled, versioned class-weighted diagnostic that uses inverse-frequency weights during training without overwriting the historical frozen baseline. See [`../configs/training/centralized_cnn_weighted_v1.json`](../configs/training/centralized_cnn_weighted_v1.json).
- The privacy gate is documented explicitly before any new DP/HE experiment: it records the privacy unit, threat model, key ownership, noise placement, and release assumptions, and it requires written approval before any new privacy experiment. See [`privacy_approval_gate.md`](privacy_approval_gate.md).

These additions do not change the historical experiment results or imply that the supervisor's 90% target is met; they refine the evidence base for the remaining research and approval decisions.
